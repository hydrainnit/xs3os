## Movie data (TMDB)

Movie artwork, ratings and descriptions come from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org). This product
uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. You supply your own free TMDB key in the
app; it is stored encrypted on the device and never leaves it except in requests to TMDB.
